# Harbor Skiff Bilge Pump — Service Log

Entries are newest first. The log records what was done to individual hulls; it does not change the
specification.

## Hull 114 — 2031-10-11

Relief valve reset from 12 psi to 18 psi per Revision B. Diaphragm inspected at 210 running hours
and found serviceable. Float switch cleaned; no change to its settings.

## Hull 109 — 2031-08-22

Diaphragm replaced at 1030 running hours. The unit had been operating in a silted harbor and the
diaphragm showed abrasion on the discharge side.

## Hull 102 — 2031-05-30

Fuse replaced after a nuisance blow. Measured draw at the pump was 3.0 amps at 12 volts, within the
specification.

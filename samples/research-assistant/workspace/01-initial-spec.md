# Harbor Skiff Bilge Pump — Initial Specification

Revision A, issued 2031-02-14. This is the specification the first production batch was built to.

## Pump Assembly

The bilge pump is a self-priming diaphragm unit rated for 12 liters per minute at zero head. It
draws 3.1 amps at 12 volts DC and is fused at 5 amps.

## Relief Valve

The relief valve is set at 12 psi. Above that pressure the valve opens and returns flow to the
suction side of the pump.

## Float Switch

The float switch closes at a water depth of 45 millimeters and opens again at 20 millimeters. The
hysteresis is deliberate: a narrower band caused the pump to cycle continuously in a rolling sea.

## Maintenance Interval

The diaphragm is to be inspected every 250 running hours and replaced every 1000.

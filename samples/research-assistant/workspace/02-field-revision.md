# Harbor Skiff Bilge Pump — Field Revision

Revision B, issued 2031-09-03. **This document supersedes Revision A where the two disagree.**

## Relief Valve

The relief valve is set at 18 psi. The 12 psi setting of Revision A caused the valve to open during
normal operation whenever the discharge hose ran uphill to the transom fitting, so the pump never
reached its rated flow. Every unit in service is to be reset to 18 psi at its next service.

## Float Switch

Unchanged from Revision A.

## Maintenance Interval

The diaphragm inspection interval is shortened to 150 running hours for units operating in silted
harbors. The 1000-hour replacement interval is unchanged.
